      Privacy & limits

      Independent community resource; no endorsement or affiliation is implied.

      Activating a profile can request the egress IP from ipwho.is. A proxied profile shows that provider the proxy address. A direct profile shows it your own public IP, and only after you allow that collection. The location picker starts offline. Loading the online map shares your connection’s public IP and viewed area with OpenFreeMap after explicit consent. Coordinates and all map interactions work offline. No telemetry and no remotely loaded code.

      Page-level geolocation and timezone changes are compatibility shims. They are observable by sophisticated page scripts and are not a claim of fingerprinting invisibility.
